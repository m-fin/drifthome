# Premiere game: story bible (draft, Content Writer)

## Premise
Bolt (official name) wakes alone in the cozy cabin of a drifting ship. Working body, a window full of stars, no memory of the destination. Each episode Bolt explores one step further and finds out a bit more about the ship and itself.

## Tone
Calm, curious, cozy. The cabin is home; space is the adventure. No combat, no gore.

## Narrator
A quiet, wry ship's log (Kokoro, am_michael). It only describes what actually happens in the run's event log. No invented drama.

## Recurring beats (event names)
wake → float_start → cockpit_reach → launch → [new place: nebula_enter / asteroid_near_miss / planet_approach / land / dock] → cabin_enter → window_look (theme resolves).

## Slow mystery
Who built the ship? Revealed through objects in the cabin. Never tied to claims about real people or Frog.

## Episode 1: "First Light" (8–12 min)
Wake, cabin and window, zero-g float to the cockpit, launch, nebula, an asteroid near miss, landing on planet one, docking at the station, back to the window with a tease of planet two.

## Script format for Video Creator
`event | offset_s | line`. Lines are short and finish before the next event.
