# Matthew "Frog" Finley

I'm a computer science student in Cary, North Carolina. I build games, music, and small apps.

These are some of my public projects.

## Games

- **[DRIFTHOME](https://github.com/m-fin/drifthome).** A 3D space game. Bolt wakes in the cabin of a drifting ship, floats in zero-g, and flies past a nebula and an asteroid field to the planet Ember and its sky dock. It's plain JavaScript and [three.js](https://threejs.org/), with keyboard, mouse, or a gamepad.

- **[SKYCHARGE](https://github.com/m-fin/skycharge).** An always-daytime sky game. You fly a small electric plane or a blimp over islands, villages, farms, and harbor towns, and recharge at charging stations. There's no combat. Same stack as DRIFTHOME: plain JavaScript and three.js, with a keyboard or an Xbox controller. The repo credits me and the SKYCHARGE Studio team.

## Music

- **[sonicrift](https://github.com/m-fin/sonic-rift).** Electronic music made with code. Python builds the sounds, and short browser sketches play them with [Tone.js](https://tonejs.github.io/). The Bionic Body track uses a Kokoro text-to-speech voice and is labeled as AI voice. The project is on [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/sonicrift).

- **[E♭ alto sax practice pack](https://github.com/m-fin/eb-alto-sax-pack).** Print-ready practice material for E♭ alto saxophone, in written alto pitch, on US Letter paper. The original exercises and etudes are CC0, with LilyPond sources: 24 modern-jazz etudes and 60 sight-reading etudes, plus 40 more in volume 2. Public-domain Bach, Ferling, and 1920s sax studies are in the pack too. [Browse the downloads](https://m-fin.github.io/eb-alto-sax-pack/).

- **[VEWI](https://github.com/m-fin/VEWI).** An iOS app (Swift and SpriteKit) that turns the screen into saxophone keys. You hold the keys the way you'd finger a sax, and it plays the note with a synthesized tone. Building it means adding the AudioKit 4 frameworks.

## Apps

- **[Crave Lab](https://github.com/m-fin/crave-lab).** Small static web apps, also on [GitHub Pages](https://m-fin.github.io/crave-lab/):
  - [Ride Check](https://m-fin.github.io/crave-lab/ride-check/) compares a rideshare offer (fare, miles, minutes) with car costs.
  - [Wear Check](https://m-fin.github.io/crave-lab/wear-check/) turns an Open-Meteo forecast into a short note on what to wear.
  - [BTC Board](https://m-fin.github.io/crave-lab/btc-board/) shows Bitcoin price, fees, block height, and the halving countdown, with a fullscreen TV mode.
  - [Retrograph](https://m-fin.github.io/crave-lab/calc/) is a calculator with stat tests, distributions, and graphing. It isn't affiliated with Texas Instruments.

- **[The Bionic Body](https://github.com/m-fin/bionic-body).** A slide deck and a tracker on living longer by replacing parts. The tracker rates 16 body systems from 0 (idea) to 5 (routine clinical use), with an example, a bottleneck, and dated sources for each. [Site](https://m-fin.github.io/bionic-body/).

- **[Custom Playback Speed](https://github.com/m-fin/Custom-Playback-Speed).** A Chrome extension (Manifest V3) that sets the playback speed of videos on the page. Chrome allows 0.0625 to 16. There's also a bookmarklet in the README.

- **[Total Audio Duration Calculator](https://github.com/m-fin/Total-Audio-Duration-Calculator).** A Python script that adds up the length of the audio files in one folder (not subfolders) and prints the total as `H:MM:SS`. It's for a set of files such as a multi-part audiobook. Durations come from [mutagen](https://github.com/quodlibet/mutagen).
